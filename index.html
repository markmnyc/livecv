About

I'm a Technical Team Lead at FNB (FirstRand) — one of Africa's largest financial groups — where I lead a 16-person engineering function spanning integration, data, and platform delivery. Ten years in, my focus has shifted from writing code to building the platforms and governance that let an engineering organisation move fast inside a regulated bank.

I designed and shipped a governed AI platform for the organisation: a single governed gateway, dual-mode authentication with per-agent identity, full audit logging, and per-team usage telemetry, deployed to enterprise container standards. Alongside it I authored a federated AI governance framework — an extend-never-weaken contract that business units can build on but never dilute, enforced automatically in CI, and built to hold up against POPIA, SARB, FSCA, and FICA. I've also built MCP knowledge servers that expose legacy and vendor systems as queryable context, rather than leaving that knowledge locked in tribal memory.

None of that matters if nobody uses it. I ran an enablement programme that reached 150+ engineers, which has since become standing guilds and adopted engineering standards rather than a one-off initiative. On the commercial side, I eliminated an escalating multi-million-rand vendor contract by designing and building the replacement in-house on capacity we already had. I've also established the capability that reports technology value into EXCO and CEO forums, using a Measured / Attested / Estimated grading discipline so the numbers hold up under scrutiny.
